## Register

German `du`, French `vous`, Spanish `tú`, Brazilian Portuguese `você`, Italian `tu`.

## Rules

- Times are 24-hour outside English; dates put the day first.
- Counts use the language's plural forms, or a label form ("Kanäle: 3") that reads correctly for any number.
- Keep placeholders such as `{name}` exactly as they are.
- Capital `ẞ` isn't in the bundled fonts: write `SS` in upper-case text.
- Keep key hints (`OK`, `ESC`, `▲▼`) as they are; only translate the words.
